Numerical HEXACO triplet example from autoFC 0.2.0.1001

Source authors: Mengtong Li, Tianjun Sun and Bo Zhang (autoFC).
Study: Li, M., Zhang, B., Li, L., Sun, T., and Brown, A. (2025).
Mixed-Keying or Desirability-Matching in the Construction of Forced-Choice
Measures? An Empirical Investigation and Practical Recommendations.
Organizational Research Methods, 28(2), 296-329.
https://doi.org/10.1177/10944281241229784

triplet_example_data and triplet_block_info objects distributed in autoFC

Original source package, including its data and documentation:
https://cran.r-project.org/src/contrib/Archive/autoFC/autoFC_0.2.0.1001.tar.gz
Original study repository: https://osf.io/yvpz3/

Changes: convert the published R data objects into numerical CSV and normalized
PyFCIRT responses; assign person IDs from example row order; use B1-B5 block
labels and pipe-separated comparison labels; attach TIRT and MOLE declarations;
provide nested 50- and 100-person subsets. Values, statement IDs, trait labels
and source keying are retained. No responses are simulated or imputed.

source_responses.csv.gz and source_design.csv retain the complete numerical
source example in editable CSV form. scripts/prepare_hexaco_triplets.py in
PyFCIRT regenerates the converted cohorts from these files without R.
No autoFC analysis functions are included. Metadata records source hashes.
